The Problem

Identification isn't resolution.

Every health system can flag a social need. Almost none can confirm it was resolved. That gap is where outcomes are lost — and where HealthLink Compass lives.

What's breaking down
The System Today
  • Screeners are inconsistent — skipped, free-text, or never completed
  • Referrals sent to CBOs with zero confirmation of receipt
  • Care team never knows what happened after the referral
  • SDOH data is unstructured and unusable for reporting
  • High-risk patients fall through the gap between identification and action
The clinical consequence
What It Costs
  • Avoidable ER visits tied to unresolved food insecurity and transportation barriers
  • Missed prenatal appointments, worsening maternal outcomes
  • GDM complications driven by food access — not clinical failure
  • VBC contract penalties on preventable readmissions
  • Zero ROI visibility on community benefit spend

Our Solution

One workflow.
Every step.

HealthLink Compass integrates with your EHR to connect the three things that actually move outcomes: identifying needs, routing to the right resources, and confirming resolution.

01

Identify Needs

Standardized screener embedded in your EHR — in-visit, device, or patient-initiated. Structured Z-codes written back to the chart automatically.

02

Refer Resources

Live CBO directory filtered by fee, proximity, language, and eligibility. Closed-loop referral sent with patient confirmation — tracked inside the chart.

03

Track Outcomes

Network Champions confirm service delivery. 30-day follow-up surveys capture patient-reported outcomes. Resolution rates and ROI visible in one dashboard.

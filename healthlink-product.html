How It Works

Three steps. One closed loop.

Integrated directly into your EHR — so referrals don't disappear after they leave the chart.

01
📋
Identify Needs
Standardized SDOH screener embedded in-chart. Z-codes captured at the point of care. Three delivery modes — in-visit, device, or patient-initiated.
02
🔁
Refer to Resources
Live CBO directory filtered by need, proximity, fee, and language. Referrals route directly to partners — status visible inside the chart.
03
📈
Confirm Resolution
Network Champions at CBOs confirm intake and close the loop. 30-day follow-up generates resolution rates and outcome scores — automatically.

Platform Architecture

Three layers. One platform.

Each layer solves a distinct problem in the SDOH resolution chain — together, they close the loop end to end.

1
HealthLink Platform
Solves the workflow problem. Screener, referral routing, status tracking, and care team messaging — embedded inside your EHR via SMART on FHIR. No new system to log into.
EHR Embedded
2
HealthInsights Portal
